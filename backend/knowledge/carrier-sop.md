# Carrier Exception and Delay SOP

## Weather and network disruptions
For a weather disruption or network delay, verify the most recent carrier scan and the published service commitment before assigning a delivery failure. Record the carrier exception code, location, and timestamp as evidence. Notify the customer when the delivery commitment changes.

## Lost shipment trace
Open a carrier trace when tracking has no movement beyond the carrier's investigation threshold or the carrier confirms a lost parcel. Do not classify a package as lost based only on a missed delivery estimate.

## Service-level escalation
Escalate to the carrier relationship owner when a shipment exceeds its service-level commitment. Preserve promised and actual milestone timestamps in the case report.
