# Warehouse Fulfillment SOP

## Picking and packing
Compare the order line, pick confirmation, pack scan, and exception record before assigning a warehouse pick or packing failure. A completed pick scan is supporting evidence, but does not alone prove the correct item was packed.

## Damaged product
Record whether damage was reported before carrier handoff or after delivery. Preserve inspection notes and packaging evidence; distinguish warehouse damage from transit damage when evidence permits.

## Customer escalation
For an unresolved fulfillment exception, document the customer impact, the next responsible team, and the next update time. Compensation or refund commitments require an authorized reviewer.
